Common questions

Quick answers.

Another subscription, in this economy?+
Free to start, free to chat every month. The upgrade runs about one Sweetgreen a week — and it replaces the planner app, the styling guesswork, and the cost of being the one who holds it all together.
Is this just ChatGPT in a dress?+
She remembers you and she books — flights, classes, calendar. A chat box gives you homework; she hands you confirmations.
I could do this with reminders and Notion.+
You could. But a system you maintain is another job. She's the one doing the maintaining.
What happens to my data?+
Plain answer: your chats, photos, and saved items are encrypted, never sold, and never used to train models. Export or delete everything in one tap. The long version is on the privacy page — it reads like this answer, not like legalese.
I'll forget to cancel.+
Cancel anytime, two taps, from inside the app. We'd rather say it here than have you find out the hard way.
Is it on Android?+
iPhone first. Android after we get her right — drop your email below and she'll text you the day it lands.
